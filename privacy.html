Privacy policy

How ArkcosPokemonNotif handles information for its private, friends-only text notification program.

1. Information we collect

We may collect your mobile phone number, name or nickname, the date and method of your consent, your opt-in and opt-out requests, and message delivery records. Alerts may also retain links to the public posts that triggered them.

2. How we use information

We use this information only to operate ArkcosPokemonNotif, send requested queue alerts, honor STOP and HELP requests, troubleshoot delivery, prevent duplicate messages, enforce message limits, and keep evidence of consent.

3. Mobile information and consent

Mobile numbers, SMS opt-in data, and messaging consent will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

4. Service providers

We use service providers only as needed to operate the program. Twilio processes phone numbers and message-delivery information to transmit SMS messages. Hosting and source-monitoring providers may process limited technical information required to run the service. These providers act on our behalf and are not authorized to use SMS consent for their own marketing.

5. Retention

We retain active subscriber information while you participate. We may retain consent, opt-out, security, and delivery records for a reasonable period afterward to document compliance, resolve disputes, and prevent messages after an opt-out. Information is removed or de-identified when it is no longer reasonably needed.

6. Your choices

Reply STOP to any program message to unsubscribe. Reply HELP for assistance. An opt-out stops future alerts, although limited consent and suppression records may be retained so the opt-out can be honored.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards appropriate for a small private service. No storage or transmission system is completely secure, and message delivery also depends on telecommunications providers.

8. Children

ArkcosPokemonNotif is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

9. Changes

We may update this policy as the program changes. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are published. If a change materially affects SMS consent or message subject matter, we will request renewed consent when required.

10. Contact

For privacy or program questions, reply HELP to an ArkcosPokemonNotif message. Do not post your phone number or other personal information in a public forum.
